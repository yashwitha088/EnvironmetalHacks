ABOUT FIRSTFLUSH

How the estimate works

When rain follows a dry period, runoff can carry accumulated material from roads and open surfaces into drains. FirstFlush uses a transparent relative score to help prioritize limited inspection time.

Risk scoredry spell 25% + rain 20% + catchment 15% + traffic 15% + construction 10% + waste 10% + water-body sensitivity 5%

This is an estimated prioritization score. It is not a lab test, a toxicity measurement, or an official municipal alert. Coverage is incomplete and observations must be verified.

Data labels

  • Fallback: seeded records used when live providers are unavailable.
  • Field observation: user-submitted information awaiting or completing verification.
  • Model estimate: calculated from the visible factors above.
